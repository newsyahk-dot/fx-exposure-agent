# User test — guide (Nov 7–10)

(Korean version: [user-test.ko.md](user-test.ko.md))

Goal: hear from 3–5 people who invoice clients in other currencies, in time to quote one or two of them on Devpost
(only with their written OK). About 15 minutes each.

**Who:** freelancers or small sellers who are paid by clients abroad (any tool: PayPal, Wise, bank transfer, invoices).

**What to send them** (the access code goes in a separate message, never in this repo):

> Hi — I built a small tool for freelancers paid by clients abroad and would value 15 minutes of your honest reaction.
> Link: https://fx-exposure-agent.vercel.app — it is a PayPal *sandbox* demo, no real money moves.
> 1. On **Today**, read the top of the page: do you understand what it says Maya is owed, and what is late?
> 2. Open a **"Chase a late payment"** card and read the e-mail the agent wrote. With the access code, click
>    **Approve: send reminder**, then **Simulate client payment** and watch the top of the page.
> 3. Read one **"Ask to be paid … sooner"** card. Would you ever offer a client a discount like that?
> 4. Optional: at **Try it with your own invoices** (/try), paste a list of your own open invoices
>    (or click *Load a sample*). Nothing is stored. Does what it shows match how you think about your invoices?
> Then answer the questions below — short answers are fine.

## Questions (send as a form or ask in a call)

1. What do you do today when a client abroad pays late? How much time does it take you?
2. Did the Today screen tell you something you would not have known otherwise? What?
3. Would you send the reminder the agent wrote, as is? What would you change?
4. The early-payment offer: useful, or would you never use it? Why?
5. Did anything confuse you, look wrong, or feel untrustworthy?
6. If this worked on your real PayPal account, would you use it? What would stop you?
7. One sentence you would say about it to another freelancer.
8. May I quote your answers on the hackathon page (Devpost)? How should I name you — full name, first name and
   role ("Jin, illustrator, Seoul"), or anonymous ("a freelance designer")?

## After each test

- Write down answers in their words; do not polish quotes.
- Note anything that looked like a bug (screen, time, what they clicked) — fixes are possible until Nov 10.
- Only quote people who said yes to question 8, with the name form they chose.
- In Devpost "User tests", report what they said — including criticism — and what you changed because of it.
